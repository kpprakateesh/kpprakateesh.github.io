House building consultancy

For families building a house, the risk is rarely the design — it is the coordination. We act as your representative through approvals, tendering and construction, so decisions are made with drawings and costs in front of you.

What's included

  • Feasibility, setbacks and floor-plan studies
  • Coordination with architects and structural engineers
  • Plan approval and statutory paperwork support
  • Bill of quantities and contractor comparison
  • Material specification and sample approvals
  • Weekly site inspection and progress reports
  • Bill checking before you release payment

You receive

  • Working drawings and specification book
  • Contractor comparison sheet
  • Construction schedule and cash-flow plan
  • Inspection reports with photographs
  • Completion and snag closure report

Modular kitchens & storage

A kitchen is engineering as much as design: the work triangle, the height of the counter for the person cooking, where the oil goes, where the waste goes. We plan all of it before the first module is cut.

What's included

  • L, U, parallel and island layouts
  • Ergonomic counter and loft heights
  • Cutlery, spice and bottle organisers
  • Tall units, pull-outs and corner carousels
  • Chimney, hob and appliance integration
  • Water-resistant cores and edge banding

Finishes

  • Acrylic, laminate, PU and membrane shutters
  • Quartz, granite and solid-surface counters
  • Handle-less, profile and Gola options
  • Under-cabinet and task lighting
  • Optional tall-unit pantry systems

Renovation & remodelling

How we keep it livable
