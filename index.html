Ask a question
Can you fit out an office while we keep working?

Yes — that is how most of our workplace projects run. We plan the work in phases, isolate dust and noise, and schedule the disruptive trades for evenings, weekends or floor-by-floor moves. You get a written programme with dates before we start, and a single point of contact for your team.

Do you handle workstations, electrical, lighting and AC together?

We coordinate all of them under one scope: layout and seat planning, partitions and cabins, ceiling and lighting, electrical points, data containment, AC ducting and the joinery. You receive one itemised quotation rather than four contractors blaming each other.

How much does interior design cost in Chennai?

It depends on scope and specification, so we quote after seeing the space. As a guide, modular work and full-home interiors are usually priced per square foot of carpet area or per unit; consultancy-only engagements are charged as a percentage of project value. You receive an itemised quotation before any work begins, and we can work to a budget you set.

Do you take on construction, or only interiors?

Both. We provide build consultancy for new houses — drawings, approvals, contractor coordination and site supervision — and full turnkey execution where we manage every trade from foundation to furnishing. For offices, we take on the complete fit-out including civil, services and joinery.

How long does a project take?

A single room or a kitchen typically runs four to six weeks after design sign-off. A full apartment interior usually takes eight to twelve weeks. New builds depend on approvals and structure, and we give you a stage-wise schedule in writing.

Can I use my own contractor or carpenter?

Yes. We can design and supervise while your team executes, or supply drawings and specifications for your contractor to quote. Many clients start with design-only and move to turnkey later.

Do you work outside Chennai?

We take projects across Tamil Nadu on a project basis. Share the location and we will confirm feasibility and any travel costs upfront.
