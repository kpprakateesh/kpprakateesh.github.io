Company staff only

Private staff access.

The Agam vault is not published on the public internet. It opens only on devices signed in to the company Tailscale network — then the normal staff ID, password and six-digit PIN still apply.

Open the private vault Install Tailscale

Ask the office admin for the private vault address and a Tailscale invitation. It is shared privately and is intentionally absent from this public website.

01

Join the company network

Install Tailscale on your laptop or phone and sign in with the company account the admin invites you to. Use the same account on every work device.

02

Open the private address

With Tailscale connected, open the vault address your admin gave you. Outside the company network that address does not resolve.

03

Sign in to the vault

Use your staff ID, password and PIN. First time? Choose “Set up your account” and enter the one-time code from your admin.

Why it works this way

  • No public vault URL. The address is not published or linked from this site, and it is not reachable from the open internet.
  • No port forwarding. Tailscale creates an encrypted private link between approved devices across normal office and home networks.
  • Device-level gate. Only devices signed in to the company tailnet can reach the vault, and the admin can remove a lost device immediately.
  • Vault sign-in still applies. Staff ID + password + six-digit PIN, with optional authenticator (2FA). Admin and staff permissions are unchanged.
  • Records stay in the office. Encrypted data and uploaded files live on the office machine, not on the public website host.

If you cannot connect

  • Check Tailscale shows Connected and you are signed in with your company account.
  • Make sure you are using the private vault address, not the public website address.
  • If the office machine is off, the vault is unavailable — contact the office.
  • Lost phone or laptop? Tell the admin at once so the device can be removed from the tailnet and the vault sessions revoked.

Access is managed in person. There is deliberately no public sign-up, no e-mail password reset, and no way to reach the vault from an anonymous browser.
