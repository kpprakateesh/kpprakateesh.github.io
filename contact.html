Before you write

Quick answers.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. A site visit within Chennai, a discussion of scope and an indicative budget range cost nothing. Detailed drawings and 3D views are part of a paid design engagement.

How soon can you start?

We take a limited number of projects at a time. Design work usually begins within one to two weeks of a confirmed brief; execution dates are given in writing once drawings are approved.

Do you handle small jobs?

Yes — a single wardrobe, a kitchen refresh or one room. Small jobs are scheduled between larger projects.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Your location, the approximate size, a few photos of the space, your budget range and your target date. That is enough for a useful first reply.
