What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form, call, e-mail or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share — typically your name, phone number, e-mail address, project location, service required, budget range and a description of your project.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation
  • To prepare a scope, quotation or drawings for your project
  • To keep records of work agreed and completed

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Details are shared only with the people who need them to deliver your project — for example a site engineer, contractor or supplier — and only to the extent required.

Where it is stored

Enquiries sent through this website may pass through a form-handling service and arrive in our inbox. Messages sent on WhatsApp are held by WhatsApp under its own policy. Website hosting logs may record standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. It loads web fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map on the contact page; those services may receive your IP address as part of serving the content.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and, where a project proceeds, for the duration of the work and any warranty period. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your choices

Contact us through the contact page, e-mail agaminterio@gmail.com or call +91 99401 16073 to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it removed.

This notice is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. Adjust it to match how your business actually handles data before publishing.
