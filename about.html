Our story

Started to close the gap between drawing and site.

Most interior projects do not fail because of taste. They fail because the drawing was never checked against the site, the quote was never itemised, or nobody was responsible once three different trades started working in the same room.

Agam was set up to take responsibility for that whole chain — the design, the specification, the purchase and the execution. We work in small, deliberate teams so the person who draws your kitchen is also the person standing in it while it is installed.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time. When we commit to a start date, we keep it.

The team

A small studio, a workshop, and a site team.

Every project is run by a design lead and supported by a site engineer and our own carpentry workshop. Specialists — structural engineers, electricians, plumbers, painters and polish teams — are brought in per project and supervised by us.

  • Design lead — concept, drawings, client decisions
  • Site engineer — measurements, quality checks, schedules
  • Workshop — joinery and furniture fabrication
  • Trusted trades — electrical, plumbing, painting, polishing

Interested in working with us — as a client, supplier or craftsman? Write to us; we read everything that comes in.
