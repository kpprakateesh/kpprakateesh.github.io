What a project involves

The drawings behind the finish.

Good interiors are decided long before a colour is chosen. Every project we take on includes a measured survey, furniture layouts, electrical points, ceiling details and joinery drawings — the documents that keep site work predictable.

  • Measured survey of the existing space
  • Furniture layout and circulation plan
  • Reflected ceiling and lighting plan
  • Electrical and plumbing point schedule
  • Joinery sections with hardware schedule
  • Finish schedule with brand references
